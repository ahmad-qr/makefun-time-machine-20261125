# Entry notes — Funniest Time Machine Warning Label

Bounty: https://make.fun/bounty/u-eager-stork-81-9647-funniest-time-machine-warning-f8add151
Reward: $2 USDC.

The label (81 characters, one line):

> WARNING: Do not meet yourself. He is taller, funnier, and already dating your ex.

Why this label: it turns a real hazard of time travel — meeting your
duplicate self — into the joke, and saves the surprise for the last words.

The program (`main.py`, stdlib only) writes exactly this line to
`/output/entry.txt` and asserts the gate itself: 1–120 characters, not only
white space. It is deterministic — the same bytes on every run.
